The Process

A search built around you, start to close.

Not a funnel you are pushed through. A process shaped to your needs and your timing.

  1. 01

    We start with you

    A customized process, built from your specific needs and timing rather than a template. What the home has to do, and by when.

  2. 02

    Local fluency

    We share what we know about this market and where it is heading, and the detail on the region — its neighborhoods, its features, the things you only learn by living here.

  3. 03

    We sift, so you don't

    We preview homes and cut the noise, bringing you the matches worth your time — including the ones moving quietly, before they reach the open market.

  4. 04

    The right number

    A thorough market analysis of the home you want, so the price you offer is grounded in evidence, not hope.

  5. 05

    The offer, and the room

    We help you complete the paperwork and represent you in the negotiation — pressing for the best price and terms where the difference is won or lost.

  6. 06

    The right people around you

    We connect you with trusted, highly qualified providers for your mortgage, inspection, and insurance — vetted, not guessed.

  7. 07

    To the closing table

    We track every date in the contract so nothing slips, and we sit with you at closing until every question is answered and every last detail is done.
