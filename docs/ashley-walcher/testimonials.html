About these reviews

One note on attribution.

These reviews come across from Ashley's current site as written, and they arrive labelled only Buyer or Seller — no first name, no submarket. That is worth changing at the source rather than in the design.

A line like “Maggie M. · Stagecoach Buyer” proves geographic range at the same time as it proves service, and it costs nothing but a permission request. Until then the wall reads honestly as anonymous verified feedback. Ashley's Google and Zillow profiles carry the named versions.

  • Ask past clients for permission to use a first name and initial
  • Add the submarket — it is the half that does the selling
  • Note whether they were buying or selling, which is already here
  • Link the Google and Zillow profiles for independent verification
