About

Professional, timely, experienced, connected.

For over 20 years Jon has called Steamboat home. During those years he has been actively involved in real estate here in Ski Town USA. He has also worked with clients in Vail, Aspen, and Summit County; his knowledge of those markets adds value to his approach in Steamboat.

His membership on The Metzler Team adds further value — between the three of them, they have nearly 60 years of real estate experience in Steamboat.

Jon enjoys spending as much time as possible in the outdoors with his wife Julie and their lab rescues, Biscuit and Cooper. When they are not in Steamboat, chances are you can find them at the family ranch in Wyoming. He and Julie are avid cyclists and are the Race Directors for The Bear Endurance Cycling Series in Steamboat, as well as strong supporters of animal rescue programs.

Jon is a board member of the gO Initiative, a Colorado nonprofit that supports professional bike, skimo, and running teams and develops programs for those interested in making serious, long-term lifestyle changes related to their overall health and performance.
