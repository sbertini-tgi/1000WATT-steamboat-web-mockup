About

Honest, open, and prompt — from the first showing to the closing table.

Buying new property or selling your own is no trivial feat, and in a market like today's, an experienced, reliable, and communicative Steamboat real estate agent on your side is a must-have. With nearly 30 years of experience in the Colorado real estate market, on both sides of the transaction, Dean has facilitated countless home, condo, land, ranch, and commercial sales — giving personalized and attentive guidance to each and every client to ensure they meet their goals.

Clients choose Dean as their Steamboat real estate agent again and again thanks to his knowledge of the area, fast and friendly communication, and dedication to ensuring a smooth escrow process from offer to close.

Honest, open, and prompt communication is essential to a client / realtor relationship — and that's why so many of Dean's repeat clients express their appreciation of his responsiveness at any time, to answer any question, via call, text, or email. Bumps in the buying or selling road are inevitable, but are quickly navigated thanks to Dean's accessibility to his clients.

Choosing Dean as your Steamboat Springs real estate agent means you have hired someone you can trust — to give honest and straightforward advice, to guide you through a seamless purchase or sale, and to do the legwork to ensure you come out on top financially.
