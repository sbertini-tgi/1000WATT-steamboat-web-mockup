Meet Lauren

Twenty years reading land, six years selling it.

A Colorado native who left Denver for the mountains in 2021, Lauren came to real estate from an award-winning career in landscape design and horticulture — two decades spent working out what a piece of ground can and cannot become.

That is an unusual advantage in a county where the answer to “can I build here?” depends on slope, water, access and a zoning code most buyers have never read. She lives on a ranchette near Phippsburg and works out of the Lincoln Avenue office in Steamboat.

Areas of expertise

Six corners of Routt County.

All neighborhoods →

Steamboat Springs

Ski-in condominiums, Old Town Victorians and the Yampa Street riverfront. The most liquid market in the county, and the most competitive.

Stagecoach

Twenty minutes south, big views, and lots you can still buy. Read the covenants and the water taps before you fall in love with a parcel.

Oak Creek

A real town with a real main street. Where most of Lauren's land and ranchette work happens, from quarter-acre infill to eighty-acre holdings.

Phippsburg & Yampa

South Routt ranching country, and where Lauren lives. Old houses on generous lots, at prices that no longer exist further north.

Hayden

The valley's growth story — airport, new subdivisions, and the shortest commute-to-price ratio in the county.

Clark

Elk River Valley: Steamboat Lake, Hahn's Peak, and cabins that trade on privacy rather than proximity.
