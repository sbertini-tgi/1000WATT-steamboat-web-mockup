Get to know me

From landscape design to land acquisition.

The through-line is the same in both careers: understanding what a piece of ground will let you do.

Lauren Bloom is a Broker Associate and Partner with The Group Real Estate, recognised for a results-driven approach and deep expertise in the Steamboat Springs and Routt County markets. A Colorado native who relocated from Golden to Steamboat in 2021, she brings an unusual mix of professionalism, precision and patience to every transaction.

Licensed since 2020, she has closed more than 120 transactions totalling over $60 million across Routt and Mesa counties — everything from rural parcels to luxury estates. In 2025 alone she completed 36 transactions and $20 million in sales.

Before real estate, Lauren built a distinguished twenty-year career as an award-winning landscape designer and horticulturist, earning statewide recognition for transformative projects. She and her husband owned a Denver design-build firm that was featured in national publications and ranked consistently among Colorado's top landscape companies. That foundation gives her clients a rare advantage when evaluating land potential, navigating site development, or envisioning sustainable, environmentally aligned properties across the Yampa Valley.

She also maintains a real estate portfolio of her own — thoughtfully reimagined residences from Denver's Front Range to Palisade — which is the shortest explanation of how she thinks about long-term value.

Lauren specialises in land acquisition, rural development, investment properties, and South Routt single-family home and ranch sales. With an office in Steamboat and a ranchette near the quiet town of Phippsburg, she stays close to the ranching community she calls home. As a sponsor of the Steamboat Springs Pro Rodeo and a farm producer for the Community Agriculture Alliance, she is genuinely inside the community that defines Routt County.

Clients value her straightforward communication, sharp negotiation and hands-on guidance through the region's complex zoning and permitting processes. Her mission is simple: a real estate experience defined by trust, strategy and results.

Specialties

What she gets hired for.

Roughly in order of how often the phone rings about it.

Rural Routt County is not a smaller version of the Steamboat market — it is a different one, with its own questions. Water rights and well permits. Access easements across a neighbour's ground. Whether a slope will ever hold a septic field. Which covenants are enforced and which are decoration.

None of that shows up in an MLS listing, and none of it is answerable from Denver. It is the substance of the work.

  • Land acquisition — from infill lots to eighty-acre holdings
  • Rural development, subdivision and site feasibility
  • South Routt single-family homes and ranchettes
  • Investment and short-term-rental underwriting
  • Zoning, permitting and covenant review
  • Out-of-state buyers who need the ground truth
