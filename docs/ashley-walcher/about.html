About Ashley

She grew up inside this business.

Most agents learn negotiation on the job. Ashley learned it at the dinner table.

Ashley thrives on helping others discover the lifestyle she and her family love in Steamboat Springs. An outdoor enthusiast at heart, she takes what the Yampa Valley offers — snowboarding with her kids, hiking the trails, living in a working mountain community. Grateful to raise her children here, she understands how particular a place it is to call home.

Real estate is more than a career — it is a family legacy. With her father a respected Denver broker of more than twenty years, and her mother recognised among the top mortgage brokers in the nation, Ashley grew up immersed in the industry. That background shaped her instincts, her negotiation and her commitment to clients.

Since 2016 she has closed over 160 transactions representing $120 million in sales volume. Selling more than $15 million annually for the past five years, she ranks among the top 40 producing agents in Routt County. Her colleagues also elected her to serve on the Advisory Board at The Group Real Estate in Steamboat.

Clients describe her as approachable, professional and deeply invested in their success. Whether the search is for a primary home, a mountain retreat or an investment property, she brings the outdoor life, the family expertise and an outgoing, level temperament to every step of it.

Specialties

What she gets hired for.

Roughly in order of how often the phone rings about it.

Routt County is eleven submarkets pretending to be one. A buyer who reads the county median and shops on it will end up in the wrong place at the right price — or the right place two years too late. Most of Ashley's work is spent on the question that comes before the house: which of the eleven is actually yours.

The rest is the part her clients name in reviews. She opens with a straight thirty-minute conversation about use, budget and expectations, then owns the conversations with the other side so her clients do not have to.

  • First homes and move-up buyers in and around town
  • Mountain retreats and second homes at the base area
  • Short-term-rental underwriting in the Mountain Area
  • Acreage and equestrian property in the South Valley
  • Oak Creek, Stagecoach and Hayden value buyers
  • Out-of-state buyers who need the submarket picked first
