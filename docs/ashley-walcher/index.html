Steamboat Springs & Routt County.

All eleven neighborhoods →

Mountain Area

Ski-in condominiums and lodges at the base of Mount Werner. The most rentable inventory in the valley, and the most sensitive to what the resort does next.

Downtown

Old Town grid, Lincoln Avenue, the Yampa River. Walkable, tightly held, and the one part of Steamboat where a lot still trades on its address alone.

Fish Creek

Not one market but three price points on one road — a two-bedroom condo and a fourteen-acre estate share the same neighbourhood boundary.

West Steamboat & Elk River

Where the median dollar buys the most square footage and the shortest walk to a public school. Every out-of-market buyer knows that part already.

Strawberry Park

Up the valley behind town: bigger parcels, the hot springs road, and school-district addresses without a subdivision feel.

South Valley

Elbow room and mountain views eleven miles out. Buying acreage here is not buying a house in town — land, access, utilities and zoning shape it as much as the building does.
