For buyers

Buying here, in four steps.

Most buyer guides run to ten steps because they are describing paperwork. This one describes the four decisions that actually change the outcome.

The process

Four decisions, in order.

The Group's full buyer process sits behind this — escrow, inspection, appraisal, closing. These are the parts where you and Ashley do the thinking.

  1. 01

    Take the thirty-minute call first

    Before any showings: what you are looking for, how you will actually use it, what it will cost to hold, and what you are willing to trade. Clients name this call in their reviews more than anything else Ashley does, because everything downstream gets faster once it has happened.

  2. 02

    Pick the submarket, then the house

    Mountain Area, Downtown, Fish Creek, West Steamboat, Strawberry Park, South Valley, Stagecoach, Oak Creek, Hayden, North Routt, South Routt. Eleven different trades between price, commute, rentability and school district. Shopping across all of them at once is the most common way buyers lose a season.

  3. 03

    Decide your number, not your approval

    A lender will pre-approve you for more than you want to spend. Set the monthly payment you are comfortable with, then get pre-qualified against it — and budget the things a Front Range buyer forgets: plowing, propane, HOA dues that in some complexes carry the water tap, and a rental licence that may or may not transfer.

  4. 04

    Write an offer the seller believes will close

    Price is one term of several. Inspection window, financing contingency, closing date, post-closing occupancy, what stays with the house. Ashley owns the conversations with the other agent so you are not negotiating against your own listing agent's read of you.
