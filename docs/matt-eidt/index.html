Three kinds of work

Residential, commercial, investment.

Most Steamboat agents do one of these. Doing all three is why Matt gets called about the odd ones.

Steamboat's commercial and mixed-use inventory is small, rarely marketed widely, and priced on assumptions that are hard to check from outside. A downtown retail bay, a Copper Ridge industrial unit, a short-term-rental condominium underwritten on real occupancy rather than projected — these need someone who has actually closed them.

The residential half is the volume: first mountain homes, move-ups, second homes and luxury. Roughly half of Matt's recent closings were on the buy side, working against other brokerages' listings, which is worth knowing if you are the one buying.

  • Downtown and Lincoln Avenue retail and office
  • Short-term-rental underwriting on real occupancy
  • First mountain homes and move-up buyers
  • Luxury and second-home sellers
  • Commercial and mixed-use, including Copper Ridge
  • Buy-side representation against other brokerages
