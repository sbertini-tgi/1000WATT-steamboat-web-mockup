For sellers

Selling here, in four steps.

One of Lauren's clients had a house sit for seven months and two showings with another agent, then took an offer within a month of relisting. Almost all of that difference is made in the first three weeks.

The process

Four decisions, in order.

  1. 01

    Be honest about why you are selling

    Speed and price are different goals and they need different strategies. Tell Lauren which one you actually care about — a seller who needs to be out by October is priced differently from one who wants a number, and pretending otherwise costs both.

  2. 02

    Price it against sales, not hopes

    Overpricing loses the first three weeks, which are the weeks with the most attention. Then every reduction reads as a problem. Lauren will show you the comparable closings, tell you what she thinks the property does, and be direct if the number you want is not in the data.

  3. 03

    Prepare only what returns money

    Declutter, depersonalise, fix the small broken things, deep clean. Beyond that, the list is short and specific to your property — and for land, preparation means a clean survey, a current well report and a mowed access, not staging.

  4. 04

    Spend the marketing in the first three weeks

    Photography, video, the MLS write-up, agent-to-agent outreach, targeted social, and the short list of buyers already looking for exactly this. Front-load all of it. A listing that is still getting first impressions in month three has usually already lost.

Start here

Start with a number you can trust.

Before any of the four steps, you need a realistic value. An automated estimate is fine as an anchor and useless as a strategy.
