Content Mapping Guide

Three companion documents that plan The Group's move to the new 1000WATT design across two markets. Two of them map a current site onto the new mockup; the third compares the two current sites to each other. Start here, then open whichever you need.

Covers the Steamboat site (thegrouprealestatess.com) and the Fort Collins / NoCo site (thegroup-fortcollins-cms.mytribus.com) · new mockup index.html · Prepared Aug 17, 2026

01The three mappings

Each card links to a full report and lists its orphans — the content the report flags as having no home in the target design. Orphans are where the decisions live, so they're pulled to the surface here.

Steamboat → 1000WATT

steamboat-to-1000watt-mapping.html

Maps every bucket of the current Steamboat production site (thegrouprealestatess.com) onto the new editorial homepage — what moves in directly, what becomes a deeper page, what must be rewritten into the new voice, and what has nowhere to go. The original framework the other two are modeled on.

Orphaned items — no slot in the mockup

  • Property search & live listings — IDX, Open Houses, Sold Homes, saved-search/Account (homepage leads with buyers, not listings)
  • Featured Properties carousel — the buyers-not-listings inversion to confirm
  • Commercial Sale & Lease — no tone-appropriate slot
  • Our Communities — Yampa Valley neighborhood guides (SEO/buyer-intent)
  • Property Management & Vacation Rentals — resort service line
  • Mortgage & Forecast — finance/utility content
  • About Us — no dedicated About page in the design

Fort Collins Tribus → 1000WATT

noco-to-1000watt-mapping.html

The Fort Collins counterpart — same method, same target design, the larger source site (mytribus.com). Maps the NoCo brokerage's 30-odd destinations onto the mockup, noting where it diverges from Steamboat: a 277-broker roster, a whole Market Data library, and a recruiting track.

  • Mortgage & property-search stack — Group Mortgage, IDX, Log In, Featured listings

02How the three fit together

Read them in whatever order suits the question, but this is the logic that connects them.

Two "→ 1000WATT" maps

One per market. Each answers: what happens to this site's content in the new design, and what's orphaned by it.

One "↔" comparison

Steamboat against Fort Collins. Answers: how much is shared, so how much of the rebuild can be a single template.

Same rubric, same shape

All three use the same five-chip color system and matching section order, so rows line up across documents.

Orphans are the agenda

Every orphan above is a decision — retire, build a deeper page, or keep per-market. That list is the real backlog.
