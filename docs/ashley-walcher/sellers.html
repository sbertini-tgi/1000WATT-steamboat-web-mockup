For sellers

Selling here, in four steps.

One of Ashley's sellers had buyers demand an abbreviated transfer timeline and still closed clean. That is what the preparation is for.

The process

Four decisions, in order.

  1. 01

    Be honest about why you are selling

    Speed and price are different goals needing different strategies. A seller who has to be out by October is priced differently from one who wants a number. Tell Ashley which one you actually care about — pretending otherwise costs both.

  2. 02

    Price against your submarket, not the county

    The Routt County median is an average of eleven places that behave nothing alike, and the portals price off the aggregate. Ashley will show you the comparable closings inside your own submarket, and be direct if the number you want is not in them.

  3. 03

    Prepare only what returns money

    Declutter, depersonalise, fix the small broken things, deep clean. Beyond that the list is short and specific to your property — and for a rental unit it includes the occupancy history and licence paperwork a buyer's lender will ask for anyway.

  4. 04

    Spend the marketing in the first three weeks

    Photography, video, the MLS write-up, agent-to-agent outreach, targeted social, and the short list of buyers already looking for exactly this. Front-load it. A listing still getting first impressions in month three has usually already lost.
