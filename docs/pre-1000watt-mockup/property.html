The property

Unobstructed mountain views on a rare 2.53-acre estate.

The perfect blend of everyday comfort and the ultimate workshop. At the heart of this beautifully appointed home is a spacious main-floor primary suite with a five-piece bath and walk-in closet. The kitchen — gas range, stainless appliances, quartz countertops, hardwood floors — flows into a vaulted great room framing stunning mountain backdrops.

For the hobbyist or RV owner, the pristine 1,600 sq ft outbuilding features two 14-ft high-bay doors, 30/50-amp outlets, and a concrete floor, while the attached three-car garage includes an extra-deep bay. A main-floor office, two upstairs bedrooms, and a basement with a fourth bedroom, full bath, and vast unfinished expansion space complete the picture. Updated systems throughout — you truly get it all.
