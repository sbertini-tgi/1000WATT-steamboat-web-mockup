About

From Varna to the Yampa Valley.

Martin was recognized by Real Trends in 2023 as one of America's Best REALTORS®, ranking in the top 1.5% of more than 1.6 million licensed Realtors nationwide based on 2022 sales — #42 for sides and #126 for volume among individual agents.

Martin was born and raised in Bulgaria in a family with very scarce resources. After completing his coursework in marketing at the University of Economics in Varna, he moved to the United States. In 2000 he arrived in New York City filled with enthusiasm, enchanted by the skyscrapers, and carrying a $100 bill in his pocket — but he had brought with him everything he needed: his past had taught him diligence, and his present offered him the chance to follow his dreams.

A year later, in May 2001, Martin moved to Steamboat Springs and immediately fell in love with the friendly community and the magical beauty of the Yampa Valley. The famous Yampa Valley curse — once a person visits the valley, they will forever be compelled to return — got him too. For Martin it wasn't a curse, but an amazing place to call home.

Martin's whole career has been in customer service and sales, building on his marketing background with everyday experience. He has learned how to identify people's needs and offer them the best of every situation. In 2009 he was selected for Leadership Steamboat, a year-long program for professionals who become experts in the local economy. He specializes in residential and resort real estate, and in guiding young families through the process of buying their first home.

In his free time Martin enjoys soccer, tennis, hiking, mountain biking, fishing, golfing (trying), and snowboarding. Working and playing in Steamboat Springs with his wife Kremena and their twin daughters, Gabby and Izzy, has been a privilege.
