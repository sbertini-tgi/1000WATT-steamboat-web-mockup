The property

Six acres in Catamount Ranch, looking straight up Walton Creek Canyon.

Privately positioned in a quiet setting in Catamount Ranch, this exceptional six-acre estate captures one of Steamboat's most distinctive view corridors, extending up Walton Creek Canyon toward June Falls and the Steamboat Ski Area, with additional vistas across Emerald Mountain, the Flat Tops, and the surrounding valley.

Set adjacent to Catamount's 12th green, the home welcomes morning light, while the ridge behind the property provides afternoon shade and protection across the many indoor and extended outdoor living spaces. Pristine lawns, mature trees, and natural landscaping create a peaceful, park-like setting with remarkable privacy.

Timeless Craftsman architecture has been reimagined with a refined contemporary sensibility, complemented by extensive updates throughout — beautifully renovated bathrooms, a remodeled primary bath, refinished flooring, and more. The residence offers six ensuite bedrooms, seven bathrooms, two powder rooms, and is designed for a future elevator installation. The main level flows easily between the well-appointed kitchen, great room, dining room, office, and primary suite.

The lower level is ideal for guests, with a family room leading to a patio with hot tub, a spacious bedroom suite, steam shower, gym or bonus room, and remodeled laundry room, while the upper level offers additional private guest quarters and a loft. An oversized three-car garage includes an EV charger. The property sits three paved miles from Steamboat and the Steamboat Ski Area, within Catamount Ranch and Club and its Tom Weiskopf-designed golf course.
