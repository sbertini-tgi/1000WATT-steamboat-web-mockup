The property

Aspen and pine, city green space behind, and the ski area out front.

2175 Bear Dr. is tucked into one of Steamboat Springs' most peaceful neighborhoods: Running Bear. This mountain home sits on a generous 1.21 acres of aspen and pine forested land and backs to city-owned green space. Enjoy elevated views of the Steamboat Ski Area, stunning west-facing sunsets, and direct backyard trail access for seasonal hiking — minutes from world-class skiing and base area events, the free-flowing Yampa River, eclectic boutiques, and convenient shopping.

The home offers 5,039 square feet and showcases locally handcrafted cedar trim, cedar tongue-and-groove ceilings, cedar-clad windows, and two fireplaces. There are five bedrooms, two of them on the main floor, and 5.5 bathrooms. The new main-level bedroom has an ensuite bath and direct access to the back patio, for optimal single-floor living; the second main-floor bedroom has vaulted ceilings and westerly views.

Upstairs, a spacious vaulted primary suite with an ensuite bath captures sweeping western views, alongside a flexible loft office or media room and a bonus room with a large closet. Kitchen highlights include a long center island, solid cherry cabinetry, granite countertops, and new Bosch appliances. The generous dining area looks out to the backyard, where elk, deer, the occasional bear, and abundant birdlife make for memorable dining companions.

The lower level offers a family and recreation room, two bedrooms with private baths, an unfinished area perfect for a wine room or private gym, and a mechanical room. A three-car garage with built-in cabinetry, wrap-around decks, and a flagstone patio with a hot tub complete this well-crafted mountain retreat in one of Steamboat's most established neighborhoods.
