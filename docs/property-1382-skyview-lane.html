The property

One of the last multifamily development sites inside city limits.

Multifamily development opportunity on 1.49 acres in Steamboat Springs — one of the last available opportunities within Steamboat Springs city limits. Zoned MF-3, the property may offer significant potential for a larger multifamily project.

With no HOA, access to the free Steamboat bus route, a direct connection to the trail system, all utility infrastructure already in place, and a position near the ski area, this opportunity offers a prime location for development. An established and successful apartment rental complex next door makes the location conforming and suitable.

The existing duplex dwelling could be incorporated into a future development and provides flexibility for a buyer who is not ready to develop immediately — including the option to use the property as a long-term rental while planning a future project, all while taking advantage of the lower residential tax rate.

1382 Skyview Lane is fully finished with three bedrooms, two baths, approximately 1,500 square feet and a two-car garage. 1384 Skyview Lane offers approximately 2,500 square feet with three bedrooms plus a mother-in-law suite, three baths, an oversized deck with excellent views, and a three-car garage. An adjacent ±0.34-acre parcel with an existing home is separately available for purchase, creating the potential for a ±1.83-acre assemblage.
