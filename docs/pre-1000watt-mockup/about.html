Our story

We measure ourselves differently.

The Group was founded in Colorado in 1976 by people who believe that real estate practiced with genuine discipline produces something volume alone never can. Every agent is full-time, to ensure full focus and dedication to the profession. We combine intimate knowledge of the market with an unwavering commitment to the client and community — building relationships that outlast transactions.

We are employee-owned, which means the people who serve you have a stake in the entire company's reputation that goes beyond their next commission. We invest in our agents so they can invest in you.

Our community matters.

Our agents aren't relocating to serve this market. They're raising families here, serving on local boards, coaching youth sports, and showing up at city council meetings. That's not a marketing statement — it's the practical reason our market knowledge runs deeper than a data report.

In 2013 we established GroupGives, a fund of the Community Foundation of Northern Colorado supported by Group agents, staff, and affiliated businesses. To date we've contributed over $1.6 million back to the communities we serve.
