The Property

Steamboat Airport Industrial Park is ideally located just one mile north of Highway 40 along Elk River Road (County Road 129) with multiple access points.

This new subdivision features 27 lots ranging from 0.25 up to 3 acres. Whether you’re looking to expand an existing business, start a new one, or simply build a shop for all your equipment, we have the perfect space for you to build on. This is truly one of the last opportunities in Steamboat Springs to own industrial land. Permitted uses are extensive and flexible, including: live/work, workforce housing, automotive repair/sales/rentals, contractor shops, equipment storage yards, food & beverage processing, heavy equipment repairs/sales, manufacturing of all kinds, office/flex spaces, nurseries, mobile food trucks shops, restaurants & tasting/tap rooms, tech and utility centers, warehouse & storage for your business of toys and many more.

This subdivision represents a rare chance to meet Steamboat’s growing demand for commercial and industrial services. See Supplemental Documents for the plat and site plan. Final Plat and construction Drawings have been approved and construction will commence asap spring of 2026 and estimated completion of fall of 2026. Zoning is Industrial.
