The Property

Multifamily development opportunity on 1.49 acres in Steamboat Springs - one of the last available opportunities within Steamboat Springs city limits!

Zoned MF-3 the property may offer significant potential for a larger multifamily project. With no HOA, access to the free Steamboat bus route, direct connection to the trail system, all utility infrastructure already in place, and ideally positioned near the ski area, this opportunity offers a prime location for a development. Established and successful apartment rental complex next door makes the location conforming and suitable.

Existing duplex dwelling could be incorporated in future development and provides flexibility for a buyer who is not ready to develop immediately, including the option to use the property as a long-term rental while planning a future project all while taking advantage of the lower residential tax rate. 1382 Skyview Lane is fully finished with 3 bedrooms, 2 baths, approximately 1,500 square feet and a 2-car garage and 1384 Skyview Lane offers approximately 2,500 square feet with 3 bedrooms plus a mother-in-law suite, 3 baths, an oversized deck with excellent views and a 3-car garage.

An adjacent approximately 0.34-acre parcel with an existing home is separately available for purchase, creating the potential for an approximately 1.83-acre assemblage. This opportunity offers a unique combination of acreage, existing improvements, location and multi-family zoning!
