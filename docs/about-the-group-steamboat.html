About The Group

A leading firm, built on client satisfaction.

The Group Real Estate is a full-service brokerage headquartered in Steamboat Springs, with a team of more than eighty brokers and roughly a quarter of the market — a share earned through an unwavering focus on the client.

Each year we help over a thousand clients across Routt County, and we rank consistently among the top firms in the valley. That record comes from a relentless attention to the client experience: innovative technology, sharp marketing, strong in-house systems, and relationships that reach around the globe.

Two Firms, One Philosophy

Colorado roots, joined in 2022.

On August 1, 2022, Colorado Group Realty joined forces with The Group — a powerhouse in Northern Colorado real estate since 1976. Two like-minded firms, now one.

Both are affiliates of Leading Real Estate Companies of the World, and both practice Ninja Selling — the sales philosophy created by The Group's co-founder, Larry Kendall. The names came together because the values already had.

Why The Group

We apply all we know to help you go from the life you have to the life you dream about.

Buying or selling a home is one of the most significant financial decisions most people ever make. The professional you choose matters. Where many agents handle only a handful of sales a year, our brokers work at a far higher volume — and bring that experience to every transaction.

Still, the business is built on relationships and trust. Our relationship with you matters more than the money or the deal. Two decades of deep local roots, and a real commitment to giving back through GroupGives Yampa Valley, are why choosing a Group broker is the clear call in this market.
