The Property

Elevated mountain living begins at 1240 Hawks Nest Lane, where sweeping views, unforgettable sunsets and thoughtful design create an exceptional home in one of Gypsum's most convenient locations.

Natural light fills the open-concept main level, connecting the kitchen, dining and living spaces with an effortless flow. A private first-floor bedroom with its own en-suite bathroom creates the perfect mother-in-law suite or refined guest retreat, while a separate half bath serves the main living area. Upstairs, the primary suite becomes a private mountain retreat with beautiful views, multiple closets and a luxurious Euro-style shower.

The adjoining flex space offers room for a sophisticated home office, fitness studio, media lounge or second living area. Generous kitchen storage and additional crawl-space attic areas ensure everything has its place. Solar powers the home, while air conditioning and forced-air heating provide dependable comfort through every Eagle County season.

The large two-car garage delivers the space local buyers want for vehicles, skis, bikes, snowmobiles, tools and all the gear that comes with mountain living. Outside, the fenced yard and established garden create a private setting for entertaining, relaxing and watching the Colorado sky come alive at sunset. Trails, parks, schools, the Gypsum Recreation Center, Eagle County Regional Airport, local dining and I-70 access are all just minutes away.

Offered under $750,000, this is a rare combination of luxury, flexibility, efficiency, storage and location, designed for the way Eagle County is meant to be lived.
