About me

Built on fairness and legendary service.

His words, and after fourteen years the town appears to agree with them.

Since becoming a Realtor® in 2012, Matt has built his business on fairness, integrity, and a commitment to providing legendary service. A Steamboat local since 2007, he has a deep-rooted connection to the community, and lives in downtown Steamboat Springs with his wife, young daughter and son.

His dedication to real estate has earned significant recognition: the youngest owner in the history of Colorado Group Realty (now The Group Real Estate), the prestigious Twenty-Under-40 award, and “Best of the Boat, Best Real Estate Agent” for nine years.

Beyond real estate, Matt is actively involved in the community, having served on the boards of Steamboat Creates, Group Gives Charitable Foundation, Yampa Valley Gives, and the Steamboat Springs Lions Club and Young Professionals Network.

With a business degree and a background in sales and professional photography, he brings an unusual blend of creativity and market expertise to every transaction. His understanding of Steamboat's complex market makes him a resource for buyers and sellers alike, whether residential, commercial or investment.

Whether you are purchasing your first mountain home or selling a luxury property, you won't find a more honest, effective and personalised approach.

The photography

He shoots his own listings.

A background most agents outsource, and the reason his marketing goes out faster.

Before real estate Matt worked as a professional photographer. On his own listings that means the shot list is set by someone who knows both what sells a Steamboat property and how the light behaves in these rooms in February — and that a re-shoot does not cost a week of calendar.

It also shapes how he reads other people's marketing. A listing photographed to hide something is legible to him in a way it is not to most buyers, which is worth having on your side of the table.
