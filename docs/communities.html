The valley, by neighborhood.

Steamboat is a handful of distinct places, and the Yampa Valley many more. Here is the lay of the land.

Communities

One valley, many corners of it.

Where you live here shapes how you live here. Downtown puts the hot springs and Lincoln Avenue at your door; the Mountain Area puts the gondola there instead. Head north and the valley opens into ranch land and lakes; head south and it quiets into working towns and open sky.

Below is each community, in brief. When one pulls at you, we know it well enough to tell you what the listings won't — and to find you the right place in it.

Downtown

Historic Lincoln Avenue and Old Town — walkable, warm, and steps from the Yampa River and the hot springs. In-town living at its most classic.

Elk River Area

North of town along the Elk River — ranch land, acreage, and valley-floor properties with room to breathe and big-sky views.

Fish Creek Falls Area

Established east-side neighborhoods near the falls trailhead — quick access to town on one side and the backcountry on the other.

Hayden

West down US 40 — small-town character, the county airport, wide valley views, and some of the area's more attainable prices.

Mountain Area

At the base of Steamboat Resort — slopeside homes and condos, ski-in access, and the center of gravity all winter long.

North Routt

Clark, Hahn's Peak, and Steamboat Lake — forested, remote, and lakeside. True mountain seclusion within reach of town.

South Routt

Oak Creek, Yampa, and Phippsburg — ranching towns at the valley's south end, with quiet, character, and real value.

South Valley

The river corridor south of town — a mix of subdivisions and acreage bridging Steamboat and Stagecoach.

Stagecoach

Around Stagecoach Reservoir — lake recreation, a growing community, and some of the best value in the county.

Strawberry Park

Just northeast of downtown — wooded lots, the famous hot springs, and a tucked-away feel that stays close to town.

West Steamboat & US 40

The western gateway — newer neighborhoods and the area's growth edge, minutes from downtown along the highway.
