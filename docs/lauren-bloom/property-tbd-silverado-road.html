The property

Thirty-eight acres on top of Silverado Road, with nothing in the way of the view.

A rare and exceptional offering, this 38.27-acre parcel atop Silverado Road presents a truly remarkable opportunity to own one of Routt County's most awe-inspiring settings. From its elevated perch, the property commands breathtaking 360-degree views stretching from Mt. Werner and Sleeping Giant to the distant Flat Tops Wilderness.

With agricultural zoning, no HOA, and a landscape defined by privacy, scale and natural beauty, this is an ideal canvas for a luxury mountain estate, legacy holding, or refined off-grid retreat. Offering the rare balance of seclusion and accessibility, with strong cell service and electricity nearby, this is Colorado landownership at its most elevated.
