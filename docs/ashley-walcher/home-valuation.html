You can have the automated number in thirty seconds. It is worth what it costs, which is why Ashley sends a second one behind it.

How I price

The median is not your price.

Everything Ashley writes in the journal is really about this one problem.

Automated valuation models are built on volume and they price off aggregates. Routt County does not cooperate. Fish Creek alone holds a $595,000 condo and a fourteen-acre estate inside one boundary; West Steamboat leads the county on dollars-per-square-foot while trailing it on appreciation. An algorithm reading “Steamboat Springs, CO 80487” cannot see any of that.

What moves a real number here: which three sales a buyer's agent will actually use as comparables, whether a short-term rental licence transfers and what the occupancy history shows, whether the addition was permitted, what the HOA dues include, and — on acreage — access, water and buildable envelope.

So the estimate arrives first, and then a conversation. If the honest answer is that this is a bad year to sell your particular property, you will hear that too.
